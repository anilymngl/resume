Volume I · No. 1 The Long Ridge May 2026 · Istanbul

Not a pivot.
An accumulation.

A senior engineer's nine-year arc, from retail optimization through governed analytics to applied AI evaluation — read as one continuous line.

By Anıl Yamangil Senior AI/ML Engineer Applied AI · Data · Retail

The résumé says three roles. The work says one trajectory. Retail optimization taught the discipline of decisions that cost real money when they are wrong. The Snowflake and Databricks years taught how to make those decisions reproducible at scale. The AI work now under way is the same question under a different tool: can a system be trusted, and can you prove it.

Chapter I · 2017–2022

Retail, measured.

Invent Analytics. Forecasting, replenishment, custom production decision logic. One electronics retailer: ~3% lost-sales reduction, 7%+ availability lift, ~10% inventory-turnover improvement.

Chapter II · 2022–2025

A data spine.

BlueCloud, working with JLL Technologies. Snowflake and Databricks pipelines. Governed metrics, schema standards, Tableau, reliability — the quiet years that make the loud years possible.

Chapter III · 2026–

Evaluation as craft.

BlueCloud AI/ML. Cortex Analyst evaluation (text-to-SQL over Semantic Views), Cortex Search evaluation (managed retrieval), and the Semantic View Factory — an internal modeling accelerator.

“The interesting question is never did the model answer.
It is can we prove the answer, and defend it to the business.”

On ownership. Work described here is first-person. Evaluation harnesses were built, not contributed to. Pipelines were maintained. Decisions were owned. Where ownership was shared, the wording says so.

On Cortex. Cortex Analyst is Snowflake's text-to-SQL engine over Semantic Views; Cortex Search is Snowflake's managed retrieval service. Both are tools, not the identity — a current evidence pillar, not the whole picture.

On the prototypes. RAG Glassbox, Cognos Studio, Signal Ledger are laptop-local workbenches. Not SaaS. Not production. They exist to learn things in public with no false enterprise framing.

On what's next. More applied AI systems on governed data platforms. Especially the parts where model output has to survive review by people who are paid to be skeptical.
